`dotnet test` itself. **Verdict: MERGE.** Findings:
1. **Medium, fixed.** PriceAmount was forced to 2 decimals while the line total used the full
   price, which breaks PEPPOL-EN16931-R120 for prices like 0.125. PriceAmount now keeps its full
   precision, and there is a new test (0.125 × 1000 = 125.00).
2. **Low, deferred to Slice 2 (noted in a test comment).** With BuyerReference or DueDate omitted,
   the invoice fails R003 and BR-CO-25. Required-field rules belong to validation (Slice 2a/2b).

   non-S categories will fail BR-O and BR-E rules. The fixture uses S only.
4. **Low, fixed.** An explicit JSON `null` for seller, buyer or lines got past `required` and caused
   a NullReferenceException. `OrderJson.Parse` now rejects it, and 3 test cases were added.
5. **Low, not fixed.** The nl-BE culture test would throw under invariant-globalization hosts. This
   is acceptable for now because standard SDK installs have ICU.
6. **Info, fixed.** A blank line was missing before the new RUNBOOK heading.
