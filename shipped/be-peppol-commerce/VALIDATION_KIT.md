# Validation kit: BE-Peppol-Commerce

The owner runs this; sessions never contact anyone. Readable on a phone in 5 minutes.

## One-line pitch

Peppol e-invoicing for Optimizely Configured Commerce, out of the box — the connector that lets
Belgian B2B sellers on Optimizely meet the 2026-01-01 mandate without a custom integration or a
platform migration.

## Who to talk to (role, region, where to find 10 of them)

- **Role:** technical/solution architects, IT managers, or e-commerce leads at Belgian or wider-EU
  B2B companies running Optimizely Configured Commerce (Optimizely's B2B commerce product,
  formerly Insite/Episerver Commerce). Also: consultancies/agencies that implement and support
  Optimizely Commerce for clients (the owner's own peer network).
- **Region:** Belgium first (mandate is live now), then wider EU (Peppol mandates are rolling out
  across several EU states on similar timelines).
- **Where to find 10:** the owner's own consultancy colleagues and client contacts (fastest path —
  the owner already works with Optimizely Commerce clients daily); Optimizely Partner directory
  (optimizely.com/partners) filtered to Benelux/EU partners; world.optimizely.com developer forum
  (post the problem, not a pitch, and see who responds); LinkedIn search for "Optimizely Commerce"
  + "Belgium" or "B2B commerce" job titles.

## Landing page copy

**Headline:** Peppol e-invoicing for Optimizely Commerce. Live in weeks, not months.

**Subheadline:** Belgium's B2B e-invoicing mandate is already in effect. Every other major
commerce platform has a Peppol connector — Optimizely Configured Commerce doesn't, yet. We built
the one that does.

**Three benefits:**
1. Stay compliant without leaving Optimizely — no platform migration, no custom integration
   project.
2. Send and receive Peppol BIS/EN16931 invoices through your existing ERP/AP provider (Storecove,
   Qvalia, Recommand, Billit, or your provider of choice) — no new accreditation, no new vendor
   lock-in.
3. Built by people who run Optimizely Commerce for Belgian and EU clients every day — not a
   generic EDI vendor guessing at your platform.

**Call to action:** Book a 15-minute call to see if your Optimizely setup is Peppol-ready.

## Pricing hypothesis

Anchored on the Deep Check's cited comparables (`VALIDATION.md`): Peppol AP connectivity itself
prices from free (Recommand, 25 docs/mo) through €7.50-€899/month depending on document volume
(Qvalia, Recommand, Billit, all cited with source URLs in `VALIDATION.md`). This connector adds the
Optimizely-specific integration layer on top of a customer's own AP subscription. Hypothesis,
**not yet validated with a real quote**: a flat connector licence of **€99-€249/month** per
Optimizely instance, on top of the customer's own AP subscription, comparable in shape (though not
confirmed in exact price) to how Commerce-Connections and e-invoice.be price per-platform ERP
connectors. Test this range in outreach conversation 5 below rather than publishing it yet.

## Five outreach messages

Plain text, no em dashes. Dutch versions included for BE/NL targets.

**1. Cold message** (LinkedIn/email, to an Optimizely partner or client IT contact)

EN: "Hi [name] — quick question: is your Optimizely Configured Commerce setup ready for Belgium's
B2B e-invoicing mandate? I've been looking into this and couldn't find an existing Peppol connector
for Optimizely Commerce specifically. Curious how you're handling it, or if it's not on your radar
yet."

NL: "Hoi [naam] — korte vraag: is jullie Optimizely Configured Commerce omgeving klaar voor de
verplichte B2B e-facturatie in België? Ik heb hiernaar gezocht en vond geen bestaande Peppol
connector specifiek voor Optimizely Commerce. Benieuwd hoe jullie dit oplossen, of of het nog niet
op de radar staat."

**2. Warm contact** (a colleague or existing client relationship)

EN: "You know I work with Optimizely Commerce daily — I've been looking at building a Peppol
e-invoicing connector for it, since nothing exists today and the Belgian mandate is already live.
Would any of your clients on Optimizely actually need this? Would love 15 minutes to sanity-check
the idea before I build it."

NL: "Je weet dat ik dagelijks met Optimizely Commerce werk — ik overweeg een Peppol e-facturatie
connector te bouwen, want die bestaat nog niet en de Belgische verplichting is al van kracht. Zou
een van je klanten op Optimizely dit nodig hebben? Graag 15 minuten om het idee af te toetsen
voor ik begin met bouwen."

**3. Community post about the problem** (world.optimizely.com developer forum)

EN: "Has anyone dealt with Peppol/EN16931 e-invoicing on Configured Commerce for Belgian B2B
customers? I can't find an existing connector or marketplace app for this and I'm curious how
others are solving it — custom build, or is there something I'm missing?"

**4. Follow-up** (to anyone who responded to 1-3 with interest)

EN: "Thanks for the reply — to make sure I build the right thing: which Peppol Access Point/AP
provider (if any) are you already using or considering (Storecove, Qvalia, Recommand, Billit,
other), and roughly how many B2B invoices a month would need to flow through this?"

**5. Free pilot offer** (to the strongest lead)

EN: "Would you be open to a free pilot integration on a test/staging environment once I have a
working version? No cost, no commitment — I'd just want your team's feedback on whether it
actually solves the problem before I finalize pricing."

## One-week signals (strong / weak / kill criteria)

- **Strong:** at least 2 of the ~10 contacted confirm they are actively looking for exactly this
  (an Optimizely-Commerce-specific Peppol connector), or at least 1 commits to a pilot conversation
  with a real timeline.
- **Weak:** general interest/curiosity but no one confirms an active, funded need within the next
  1-2 quarters; or contacts report they've already solved this with a manual/custom integration and
  see no reason to switch.
- **Kill:** multiple contacts report they are not on Optimizely Commerce B2B (wrong target), or
  report the mandate doesn't actually affect their invoicing flow (e.g. B2C-only, or already
  Peppol-compliant via an ERP-level integration that fully bypasses Optimizely), or explicitly say
  they would not pay for this even packaged well.
