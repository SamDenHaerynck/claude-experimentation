# UBL 2.1 schemas (vendored, unmodified)

The 15 `.xsd` files under `common/` and `maindoc/` are copied byte for byte from the OASIS UBL 2.1
OS package, http://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/UBL-2.1.zip (`xsd/` folder), downloaded on
2026-09-30. They are exactly the files that `maindoc/UBL-Invoice-2.1.xsd` and
`maindoc/UBL-CreditNote-2.1.xsd` import, directly or transitively. Each file keeps its own OASIS
copyright header.
