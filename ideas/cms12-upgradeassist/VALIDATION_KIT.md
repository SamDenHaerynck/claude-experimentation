# Validation kit: CMS12-UpgradeAssist

The owner runs this; sessions never contact anyone. Readable on a phone in 5 minutes.

Context first (day 042 refresh, `REFRESH.md`): a free MIT scanner for CMS 12 to 13 appeared on 2026-10-08 (https://github.com/adnanzameer/OptimizelyCms13ReadinessScanner). Optimizely's own tooling is archived. No scanner for CMS 11 to 12 was found, and no published price for any upgrade scanner or audit exists. So the first question for real people is not "would you use a scanner" but "which upgrade (11 to 12, or 12 to 13) is hurting you, and would you pay for something the free scanner does not do".

## One-line pitch
An upgrade copilot for Optimizely CMS codebases: scans for breaking changes, missing admin features and blocking third-party packages, and produces a prioritised work list a team can estimate from.

## Who to talk to
- Optimizely developers and tech leads at agencies and partners in Belgium, NL, DACH, Nordics; your own colleagues who have run a CMS 11 to 12 or 12 to 13 upgrade.
- In-house teams still on CMS 11 (out of support since 2026-04-10 per Optimizely).
- Find 10 via: your consultancy's Optimizely projects, Optimizely World forum threads on CMS 13 upgrades, the Optimizely Slack/Discord communities, LinkedIn "Optimizely" + "upgrade" posts.

## Landing page copy
- Headline: Know what your Optimizely upgrade will break before you quote it.
- Subheadline: Scan your CMS 11 or 12 solution and get a ranked list of breaking changes, removed features and blocked packages.
- Benefits: (1) Estimate upgrades from facts, not guesses. (2) Catches what the generic upgrade tools skip: admin-screen gaps, module manifests, package compatibility. (3) Runs locally on your code; nothing leaves your machine.
- CTA: Join the waitlist / book a 20 minute call.

## Pricing hypothesis
No comparable price found. The only priced anchor is zero (free MIT scanner for 12 to 13). Partner audits (First Line, Diagram, Blend) hide prices. Hypothesis to test, not a fact: a one-off per-solution report (single digits to low hundreds of EUR) or a per-seat licence for agencies. Ask outreach targets what an audit costs them today.

## Five outreach messages
1. Cold (EN): Hi <name>, I'm building a tool that scans Optimizely CMS solutions for upgrade blockers (removed APIs, missing admin features, incompatible packages). Are you planning or mid-way through a CMS 11/12 to 13 upgrade? I'd value 15 minutes on how you scope that today.
   Cold (NL): Hallo <naam>, ik bouw een tool die Optimizely CMS-oplossingen scant op upgradeproblemen (verwijderde API's, ontbrekende adminfuncties, incompatibele packages). Staat er bij jullie een upgrade naar CMS 12 of 13 gepland? Ik hoor graag in 15 minuten hoe jullie dat nu inschatten.
2. Warm (EN): Hey <name>, you did the <client> upgrade. What took the longest to find out, and what would you have wanted a tool to tell you on day one?
   Warm (NL): Hey <naam>, jij deed de upgrade van <klant>. Wat kostte het meeste tijd om uit te zoeken, en wat had je op dag één door een tool willen laten vertellen?
3. Community post (EN): For those who have upgraded to CMS 12 or 13: how do you estimate the work up front? Do you use the Copilot agent, the community readiness scanner, or a manual checklist? What did none of them catch?
   (NL): Voor wie al naar CMS 12 of 13 is geupgrade: hoe schatten jullie het werk vooraf in? Copilot, de community scanner of een handmatige checklist? Wat vingen ze alle drie niet op?
4. Follow-up (EN): Hi <name>, following up on my note. Even a one line answer helps: what is the biggest upgrade blocker you hit?
   (NL): Hallo <naam>, even opvolgen. Een antwoord van een zin helpt al: wat was de grootste upgradeblokkade?
5. Free pilot (EN): I'll scan one of your solutions for free and send the report in exchange for 20 minutes of feedback. Code stays on your machine.
   (NL): Ik scan gratis een van jullie oplossingen en stuur het rapport terug in ruil voor 20 minuten feedback. De code blijft op jullie machine.

## One-week signals
- Strong: 3 or more people accept a call or pilot AND at least 1 names a pain the free scanner or Copilot does not cover; or any says they would pay and gives a number.
- Weak: replies say the free scanner is enough, or interest only for CMS 12 to 13 with no gap named; 1 to 2 calls.
- Kill: 5 or more direct replies, none interested or all say the free scanner covers it (that would also be H4 evidence).
