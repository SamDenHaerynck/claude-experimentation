# Idea: Small-manufacturer RFQ / subcontractor-quote comparison tool

Source: `BACKLOG.md` #1 (added 2026-09-11 sourcing-method review, day 009).

## Concept

Lets a small manufacturer or contractor send a request-for-quote (RFQ) to multiple
suppliers/subcontractors at once and compare their responses side by side in one place, instead of
the current default of separate emails/calls per supplier and a manually assembled spreadsheet.

## Target user

A small manufacturer, general contractor, or procurement-lite operator (no dedicated procurement
software, no full ERP) who regularly needs quotes from more than one supplier or sub for a job or a
part, and currently tracks responses by hand.

## Problem claimed

Comparing quotes that arrive via different channels (email, phone, PDF) at different times, in
different formats, is manual and error-prone; a shared, structured comparison view would save time
and reduce the chance of missing a better quote or a hidden term.

## Why now / rationale from BACKLOG.md

Tests property (b) from the `RUNBOOK.md` 2026-09-07 framework: value comes from being the shared
record both the buyer and multiple suppliers touch on every purchasing cycle, not a single
generated artifact. Multi-party by construction (buyer plus several suppliers must all use it),
which is the shape the 2026-09-11 sourcing-method review now prefers over a single-user
tracker/monitor, per the new `RUNBOOK.md` entry ("the monitor/track/alert idea shape is
structurally oversaturated").
