# Backlog

Ranked pool of candidate ideas not yet started. Phase 0 picks the top entry. When an idea is
selected it moves out of this list into `ideas/<slug>/`; when generating replacements, add new
candidates to the bottom unless evidence gathered during Validate suggests otherwise.

1. **Vendor security questionnaire autofill assistant** — a tool for small B2B SaaS companies that
   stores past questionnaire answers (SOC2-adjacent, InfoSec, data handling) and auto-drafts
   responses to new procurement security questionnaires from prospects/enterprise customers.
   Rationale: security questionnaires are a well-known bottleneck in enterprise sales cycles for
   small vendors who don't yet have a dedicated compliance hire.

2. **Rent increase / notice compliance calculator** — a narrow tool for small landlords and property
   managers (1-20 units) that, given a jurisdiction and lease terms, calculates legal rent-increase
   caps and required notice periods and generates a compliant notice letter. Rationale: rent control
   and notice rules vary widely by city/state and change often, creating real legal and financial
   risk for small landlords without in-house counsel.

3. **Freelancer SOW/contract generator with e-sign tracking** — a lightweight, freelancer-specific
   alternative to heavyweight contract platforms: generates scoped statements of work from a short
   intake form, tracks e-signature status, and reminds on renewal/expiry. Rationale: solo
   consultants often use generic templates or expensive all-in-one tools (DocuSign, PandaDoc) built
   for larger teams; a narrow, cheap, fast tool may fill a gap.

4. **Git-history-to-changelog generator for indie SaaS** — ingests merged PRs/commits and drafts a
   customer-facing changelog entry or release-notes email, matching a configurable tone/template.
   Rationale: solo/indie SaaS founders ship frequently but often skip customer communication because
   writing a polished changelog entry takes more time than the fix itself.
