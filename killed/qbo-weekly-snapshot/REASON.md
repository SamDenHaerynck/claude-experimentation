# Killed: Automated weekly financial snapshot for solo/micro-business owners on QuickBooks Online

Killed in Phase 1 (Validate), day 020, 2026-09-22. Score: 13/25 (below the 16 threshold); "reason
to exist alongside what already ships" independently scored 1/5 (auto-kill on its own).

## Why

`STATE.md` flagged, as the highest-priority risk ahead of everything else, whether the sourcing
job posting's three tasks — flag overdue invoices, categorize bank-feed transactions, generate a
P&L/balance-sheet/cash-flow snapshot — were really asking for something QuickBooks Online's own
native tools don't already do. Checked directly against QBO's current help-center pages: all
three are free, native QBO features today (real-time financial reports on every plan, automatic
invoice reminders plus an AR aging report, and bank-feed rules with 2026 AI-assisted
auto-categorization). The one residual piece not covered by QBO itself — following up on *why* a
transaction is uncategorized — already has a paid third-party incumbent, Uncat ($9/month/client,
syncs to QBO/Xero), which closes exactly that loop today.

The Freelancer.com posting that sourced this idea is real evidence someone wants this *work* done
weekly, but on inspection that is evidence of demand for cheap human labor to operate QBO's
existing free tools (plus judgement), not evidence of demand for a new piece of paid software —
the same "already-recurring free incumbent" failure mode that has killed several prior candidates
in this project, now confirmed for this one directly from Intuit's own product pages rather than
inferred.

## Disclosed non-competitors not independently re-verified
