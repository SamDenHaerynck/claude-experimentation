# Idea: Rent increase / notice compliance calculator

## Concept

A narrow tool for small landlords and property managers (roughly 1-20 units) that, given a
jurisdiction (US city/state) and lease terms, calculates the legal rent-increase cap and required
notice period, then generates a compliant rent-increase notice letter.

## Target user

Small, self-managing landlords and property managers (1-20 units) without in-house legal counsel,
operating in jurisdictions with rent-control caps and/or statutory notice-period requirements
(e.g. California AB 1482, NYC rent stabilization, Oregon SB 608, Washington HB 1217).

## Problem

Rent-increase rules (percentage caps, required notice-period length, required notice content) vary
by city and state and change periodically (e.g. Oregon's and Washington's caps are recalculated
annually from CPI). A landlord who miscalculates a cap or serves notice with too little lead time
risks tenant disputes, a voided increase, or a legal/regulatory penalty.

## Why now

No specific regulatory or platform trigger identified — several state-level caps (Oregon, Washington)
are recent (2019-2023) and recalculated annually, so the underlying data changes yearly even though
the rules themselves are not brand new.
