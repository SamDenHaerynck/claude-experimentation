# Idea: Co-owned vacation property scheduling and expense-splitting

## Concept

A shared calendar plus expense ledger for a group of relatives or friends who jointly own a cabin,
beach house, or timeshare-like property, so booking conflicts and shared costs (utilities, repairs,
property tax, insurance) don't have to be tracked over group text and an ad hoc shared spreadsheet.

## Target user

A group of 2-8 co-owners (typically family) of a single recreational property — not a property
manager, not a commercial rental operator, not a single-owner vacation-rental host (that's Airbnb/
VRBO's audience, a different job).

## Problem

Co-owners currently coordinate booking via group text/shared calendar apps not built for
reservation conflicts, and split costs via a shared spreadsheet or informal Venmo requests, with no
persisted, agreed-upon ledger of who owes what and why.
