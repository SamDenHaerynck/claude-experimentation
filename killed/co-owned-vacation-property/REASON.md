# Killed: Co-owned vacation property scheduling and expense-splitting

Killed in Phase 1 (Validate), day 011, 2026-09-13. Score: 14/25 (below the 16 threshold); "evidence
of demand" independently scored 2/5, an automatic kill on its own per the routine's Phase 1 rule
regardless of total.

Full evidence, competitor research, and scoring are in `VALIDATION.md` in this directory. Short
version: this session applied both 2026-09-12 `RUNBOOK.md` corrections — a deep review-page
complaint check (not a search-summary pass) on the two previously-disclosed incumbents, and an
explicit underserved-sub-segment search before scoring "reason to exist" — via a delegated research
subagent. Both came back negative, and worse than the shallow 2026-09-11 pass suggested:

- Neither OurSharedPlace nor PlumConnect has any meaningful independent review-platform footprint
  (no G2/Capterra, 2 and 4 App Store ratings respectively, no Trustpilot page for either product
  itself) — so there's no documented complaint trail to build a wedge from, not because the
  products are well-loved, but because they're too small to have attracted independent review
  coverage at all.
- The underserved-sub-segment search surfaced **five additional direct competitors** beyond the two
  originally disclosed — CabinPals, SharedKey, Shared Holiday Homes, CalDibs, and House Matters —
  bringing the real total to seven, spanning free to $600/yr. The two narrow gaps found (PlumConnect
  is ~6x pricier than OurSharedPlace; Mercury's USD-only banking excludes non-US owners) are each
  already served by a different existing competitor (CabinPals/SharedKey undercut on price; Shared
  Holiday Homes already serves the UK market), so neither is a vacant gap.
- Real user-voice evidence for the specific differentiator (a *persisted* shared ledger/calendar,
  not a one-time cost split) was thin: one genuinely on-point real post found (a Bogleheads thread
  describing a "messy" manually-maintained carryover-balance table), against a three-real-post bar.
  Reddit was unreachable a **fourth** consecutive Validate session (2026-09-09, 09-10, 09-12,
  09-13) — already escalated per `OWNER.md`'s bar, not re-notified this session.

Notably, this idea was chosen specifically to test the 2026-09-11 sourcing-method review's
preferred shape — multi-party coordination / persisted shared state, not a single-user monitor or
tracker — because that review found the monitor/tracker/alert shape colonized across five unrelated
verticals. This kill shows the multi-party-coordination shape is *also* colonized: "shared calendar
+ expense ledger for a co-owned group" is evidently copyable and saturated regardless of the
specific niche it's applied to. See `RUNBOOK.md` for the widened entry recording this.
