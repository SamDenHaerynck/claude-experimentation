# Killed: Vendor security questionnaire autofill assistant

Killed in Phase 1 (Validate), day 003, 2026-09-05. Score: 15/25 (below the 16 threshold), and
"reason to exist alongside what already ships" scored 1/5 on its own.

Full evidence, competitor pricing, and scoring are in `VALIDATION.md` in this directory. Short
version: real demand and real willingness to pay are both there (verified pricing $250/mo-$9,600/yr
across three vendors), but the category is already saturated — a YC-backed competitor (Stacksi)
targets the exact same small-vendor gap claiming 90%+ autofill, several more well-funded AI-native
entrants already sell this (Conveyor, 1up.ai, AutoRFP.ai, SafeBase, and others found in market
scans), and a free DIY substitute (spreadsheet of past answers + general-purpose LLM) is already
reported as "as accurate as a human" by a practitioner in the target segment. No wedge identified
against either the incumbents or the free substitute.
