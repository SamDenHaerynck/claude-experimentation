# Idea: Contractor-vs-employee classification risk checker with audit-trail export

**Source**: `BACKLOG.md` #1 as of 2026-09-08 (carried in from the 2026-09-07 re-ranking).

## Concept

A short questionnaire against IRS common-law-factor tests (and, per state, the ABC test) for a
specific hire, producing a dated, exportable record of the analysis and reasoning — not just a
yes/no verdict — that a small business can keep on file if the classification is later questioned.

## Target user

A small business owner, solo bookkeeper, or small HR admin who occasionally hires 1099 contractors
and is unsure whether a specific hire should be W-2, and who has no in-house counsel to produce a
defensible written record.

## Problem

Free classification quizzes exist, but (per Validate below) none of them produce a kept, dated
document — only an on-screen answer. The official free government route (IRS Form SS-8) is slow
(commonly 6-8 months for a determination). The proposed wedge is not the classification logic
itself (that's replicable) but the retained, dated, reasoned record.

## Why now

No specific trigger claimed; this was carried into the backlog as a test of property (c) from the
2026-09-07 re-ranking (cost of being wrong high enough that an undocumented free answer isn't
trusted) — see `RUNBOOK.md` and `DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-07/08.
